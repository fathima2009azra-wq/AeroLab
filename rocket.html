FLIGHT DYNAMICS

Ideal rocket equation: Δv = Isp × g₀ × ln(m₀ / mf). This estimates ideal velocity change from propellant use; it is not the same as actual altitude or final speed.

Δv = Isp × g₀ × ln(initial mass / dry mass)

Educational model: vertical, one-dimensional ascent with constant thrust and specific impulse, variable mass, and atmospheric drag. It omits staging, wind, changing gravity, engine transients and many real launch effects.
