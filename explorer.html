Explore the disciplines.

Aeronautical Engineering

Focuses on aircraft that fly within Earth's atmosphere. Engineers study aerodynamics, aircraft structures, propulsion, flight mechanics and control systems.

🚀

Aerospace Engineering

Covers flight within the atmosphere and beyond it. Engineers work on aircraft, rockets, satellites, spacecraft, propulsion and orbital systems.

BEHIND EVERY FLIGHT

What do aerospace engineers actually do?

They turn scientific ideas into systems that can fly safely and reliably.

THE WORK OF AN ENGINEER

From ideas to flight.

📐

Design

Develop aircraft and spacecraft concepts using mathematics, physics and computer-aided design.

🧪

Test & Analyse

Use simulations, experiments and test data to understand performance and identify problems.

⚙️

Build & Improve

Work with teams to improve structures, propulsion, materials, reliability and safety.
